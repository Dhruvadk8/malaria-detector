Phases of Malaria & Staging

Our AI identifies not just infection status, but the exact developmental intraerythrocytic phase to guide treatment urgency.

Phase 1 (0 - 18h)

Ring Stage

Early signet-ring trophozoite with delicate cytoplasm loop and chromatin dot. Optimal window for standard ACT.

Phase 2 (18 - 36h)

Trophozoite

Active feeding stage with amoeboid cytoplasm and dark hemozoin pigment grains metabolizing hemoglobin.

Phase 3 (36 - 48h)

Schizont Stage

Multinucleated asexual division with 8-24 merozoites ready to rupture host erythrocyte, triggering fever spikes.

Phase 4 (Sexual)

Gametocyte

Elongated crescent or banana-shaped sexual stage infectious to female Anopheles mosquitoes.

About MalariaScope

Developed as a B.Tech Computer Science Engineering project at Parul University, MalariaScope leverages deep learning to provide accessible malaria diagnosis.

Our CNN model was trained on 27,558 images from the NIH dataset, achieving clinical-grade accuracy.

Learn More About This Project

This project is a deep learning-based malaria detection system. It uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to analyze blood smear images and detect the presence of malaria parasites. The goal is to assist medical professionals with faster and more accurate diagnosis.

Key Features:

  • 📷 Image upload and analysis
  • 🧠 Accurate prediction with trained CNN model
  • 💡 Interactive user interface
  • ⚡ Fast and responsive design
